# Typed status foundation

NeoForge owns a saved ARPG ailment backend for players and living enemies. Player rule actions and enemy ability executors share the same eligibility, application, stacking and ticking rules. `NeoForgeAilments.applyStatus(owner, target, type, damage, duration, skill)` accepts a living owner and an already calculated per-pulse damage amount. It requires a living, same-world, non-allied target and excludes self, fake players, creative and spectator players. Ignite also excludes fire-immune or wet targets.

| Ailment | Damage type | Stacking | Current interaction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bleed | Physical | One strongest application | Double pulse damage when horizontal displacement since the previous sampled tick exceeds 0.05 blocks |
| Ignite | Fire | One strongest application | Removed by wetness, including water exposure |
| Poison | Nature | Up to eight independent applications | Stronger incoming applications replace the weakest at the cap |

Damage pulses every 20 loaded victim ticks. Durations are bounded to 1–1200 ticks and each base pulse to 10,000 damage. Bleed and ignite reject weaker applications. Equal-strength applications may refresh a longer duration; stronger ones use their own duration and owner. Neither refresh nor upgrade postpones their existing pulse interval. At the poison cap, equal or weaker applications are rejected; stronger replacements start a fresh interval. A final pulse on the expiration tick is permitted. Durations shorter than the next pulse interval can expire without damage.

The schema-versioned save records type, owner UUID, originating skill, damage, remaining duration and partial pulse interval in entity persistent NBT. Corrupt, over-limit and unknown-version saves are discarded. Victim unloading and player logout pause the duration without catch-up damage. Reconnect, build refresh and datapack sync do not cleanse these statuses. Death clears them, including player death clones; a cancelled death event does not clear them prematurely. Non-death player clones, including End returns, explicitly copy the saved applications without advancing time. Ordinary dimension transfer retains the entity's data; portal-provider behavior still needs an in-game check.

Player ailment rules snapshot the ailment damage stat, its associated typed damage stat and damage-over-time stat at application. On each pulse, fire/nature resistance is evaluated for an ARPG player victim without penetration; physical bleed ignores armor. Ward and native damage hooks still apply. Dedicated damage types bypass armor, shields and hit cooldown so concurrent statuses and poison stacks do not discard one another's pulses. Ignite is tagged as fire for native fire protection/immunity. These statuses can kill, including ARPG poison; vanilla poison remains a separate mechanic.

Loaded living owners resolve from the victim's current server world. An online player owner's source and synchronous damage context retain kill attribution and the originating skill. If the owner dies, unloads, logs out or leaves that world, saved pulses continue with an unattributed source; no offline reward is queued. Becoming allied, creative or spectator suppresses eligible pulses while duration continues. Custom DoT sources skip ARPG hit, crit, block and damage-taken trigger dispatch, preventing recursive hit-trigger ailments. Kill triggers remain available. Provider-specific damage hooks can still affect the native damage call and need joint testing.

The Tower Sentinel has a 10% chance to apply bleed after an actual damaging direct melee hit, for 60 ticks at one damage per pulse (two when moving). Its native Iron's Icicle and Epic Fight combo remain provider-owned. Native Iron's burning, poison, freezing and other effects are neither converted into ARPG ailments nor reapplied by this backend. Explicit ARPG and native effects can coexist; authors should avoid deliberately assigning both versions to one ability unless that extra effect is intended.

`/arpg ailments` inspects the player's active ARPG stacks and longest remaining duration. `/arpg ailments target` inspects the nearest living entity under the crosshair within 16 blocks, clipped by blocks. Both commands also show wetness, chill buildup, freeze and freeze recovery, with freeze immunity indicated for chilled targets. They are read-only and do not require an operator. A synchronized HUD/status panel is not implemented yet.

## Wet, chill and freeze

These non-damaging effects use a separate bounded, versioned `ElementalState` save under entity persistent NBT. Existing DoT saves remain compatible. Timers pause with the victim, survive ordinary reconnect/restart and non-death player clones, and clear on death. The latest cold application retains its owner UUID and skill without creating damage, XP or kill rewards. Control applications require the same living, same-world, non-self/non-allied, real survival-player eligibility as DoTs.

| Status or interaction | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Wet | Touching water refreshes 100 ticks of wetness; it lasts up to five loaded seconds after leaving water |
| Chill | One buildup stack per explicit cold application; wet doubles this to two; each stack reduces ordinary movement speed by 15% |
| Three chill stacks | Consume chill and freeze for 30 ticks (1.5 seconds); further cold during this freeze is ignored |
| Freeze recovery | Starts at 200 ticks (10 seconds), including freeze time; additional cold can chill but cannot freeze until it expires |
| Freeze immunity | `puffish_skills:freeze_immune` entity-type tag; default Sentinel, Wither and Ender Dragon can reach two chill stacks but never freeze |
| Wet + ARPG ignite | Reject new ignite applications and remove existing ARPG ignite before its next pulse |
| Damaging fire hit + cold | Thaw ARPG chill/freeze and evaporate residual wetness; preserve freeze recovery |

`NeoForgeElementalStatuses.applyCold(owner, target, duration, skill)` is the shared explicit player/enemy ability entry point. Rime Pulse uses it instead of its previous vanilla Slowness I, with the same 50–90 tick chill duration. A dry hit creates one stack; a wet hit creates two. With the spell's ten-second default cooldown, a lone Rime cast does not automatically repeat fast enough to freeze: buildup currently rewards overlapping cold applications from cooperative casters or future authored melee/cold abilities. Refreshes retain the longer remaining chill duration. Target immunity or recovery caps buildup at two. No arbitrary native Iron's ice hit automatically creates ARPG chill, so the Sentinel's native Icicle keeps its provider freezing effect.

Ordinary movement uses one temporary movement-speed multiplier: 0.85 at one chill stack, 0.70 at two, and 0 while frozen. The modifier is removed on expiry, thaw and death, and restored from saved state while ticking after reload. Creative, spectator, fake-player and currently allied targets do not receive its movement penalty. This does not disable AI, jump/knockback, teleport, Epic Fight attacks/animation displacement, or Iron's casts; it is not a guaranteed full immobilization or interrupt. Other native movement effects can still combine with it.

Fire reactions run after positive health damage, before ARPG hit-trigger dispatch, and settle state before particle callbacks. Fully blocked, absorbed or ward-consumed damage does not thaw. Fire-tagged vanilla sources, Iron's fire school sources and unambiguously fire-only synchronous ARPG damage contexts qualify; mixed ARPG channels are not guessed. Fire can thaw through an ARPG ignite pulse without enabling DoT hit procs. Residual wetness is consumed by an accepted fire hit; subsequent hit triggers see the resulting dry state. Physical water exposure still prevents new ARPG ignite and refreshes wetness on the next entity tick. No steam damage or second health hit is emitted. Native Iron's effects are neither cleared nor converted by these reactions.

Cold applications show snowflake particles; thaw/evaporation shows a small cloud. Conditional rule evaluation adds `target_wet`, `target_chilled` and `target_frozen` from the target's saved ARPG state, without changing hit delivery or damage-channel tags. Authors can combine these tags with `melee`/`attack` or spell tags for follow-up bonuses. Native provider effects are not included in those ARPG target-state tags.

## Extension boundary

`AilmentType` defines typed identity, limits and rule tags; `AilmentState` owns immutable bounded applications and pulse scheduling. The NeoForge backend performs environment checks before stepping the state and applies the resulting typed pulses. New status/cross-effect rules should settle or consume state before external callbacks, retain attribution, and keep pulse damage out of hit-trigger dispatch. Resistance, immunity and reaction balance must be explicit rather than inferred from an Iron's school name.

Wet, bounded chill/freeze, freeze recovery/immunity, fire thaw and movement-sensitive bleed are implemented here. Shock, lightning/wet damage reactions, melee shatter, poison detonation, configurable resistance/duration scaling and native-provider status synchronization remain future work. The combat type catalog already includes physical, fire, cold, lightning, blood, holy, ender and nature; it does not automatically grant an ailment to every channel. Fabric retains the existing unsaved fallback and does not have this NeoForge backend.

## Validation

Common JUnit covers pulse/expiration boundaries, movement, strongest ownership, refresh timing, bounded poison replacement, removal, save round-trip, malformed/future saves and non-hit tags. Eight additional elemental tests cover dry/wet buildup, immune targets, freeze/recovery boundaries, no refresh during freeze, fire thaw without recovery refund, partial save/attribution, malformed states and distinct target-state tags. The pure state classes also compile locally. CI compiles the actual mapped Common/NeoForge sources and executes Common tests plus provider/data verification.

In Minecraft, test player rule applications and Sentinel melee bleeds; inspect with both commands. Check standing/moving bleed, entering/leaving water while ignited, all eight poison pulses alongside bleed/ignite, ward, resistance, native fire protection, owner-attributed deaths and rewards, allied/creative/spectator exclusions, victim and owner logout/unload separately, reconnect, restart, cancelled death, respawn, dimension transfer and datapack reload. Cast Rime once on dry and wet hostiles, then overlap two wet casts from different players: verify two stacks, a 30-tick movement freeze and a retained 200-tick recovery. Repeat on a fresh Sentinel (chill only), during recovery, at the final expiry tick, with native Iron's freezing/Slowness, and during Epic Fight animations. Inflict fire damage versus ward/absorption/block and confirm only positive health damage thaws; check particles and movement restoration after reload/death. Repeat with each provider absent and with all providers installed. Actual Minecraft behavior and combat balance have not yet been verified.
