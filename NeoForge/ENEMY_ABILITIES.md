# Enemy provider abilities

Enemy archetypes combine Epic Fight's mob attack animations/behaviors with Iron's native spells. Epic Fight owns melee hit handling, impact, stun and attack state. Iron's owns the released spell projectile, spell damage source, school power and secondary effects. L2 owns level scaling and traits. Player-only skill containers are not installed onto mobs: mob movesets use Epic Fight combat behavior definitions.

## First hybrid encounter: Tower Sentinel

The dedicated `puffish_skills:tower_sentinel` retains its existing native zombie attack/combo pools and half-health phase changes. With Iron's 3.16.3 installed it can additionally release the provider's level-I `irons_spellbooks:icicle`. No player recipe, selected class or specialization is required by this enemy ability.

It only starts against its current living survival/adventure server-player target at 4–12 blocks, with line of sight, on the ground, AI enabled, no rider, no recent hurt timer, and no active Epic Fight attack/stun. Creative, spectator, allied and fake-player targets are excluded. If Epic Fight is installed but its mob patch is unavailable, casting stays disabled. With Epic Fight absent, ordinary Husk melee remains the fallback.

The first cast is delayed at least 100 loaded entity ticks. Each attempt consumes a 200-tick cooldown immediately. A 30-tick warning shows snowflake particles and a blue boss bar labelled “Icicle: interrupt or sidestep”. The Sentinel stops navigation and locks yaw/pitch toward the target's position at the start of the warning. Sidestepping can avoid the resulting straight projectile. The warning cancels if the target changes, dies, leaves the range/line of sight, becomes ineligible, the Sentinel is hit or airborne, or Epic Fight enters a busy/stunned state. Cancellation does not refund the cooldown. At close range the enemy keeps its melee loop instead of completing a ranged cast.

At release the narrow optional adapter checks Iron's spell enabled/minimum/maximum level settings, its INSTANT cast type and native pre-cast conditions. It uses mob `MagicData` and `CastSource.MOB`, then calls the provider's pre-cast, cast and completion hooks. The provider's own Icicle code creates and aims the projectile and applies its damage/freezing behavior. No second ARPG spell damage call, bespoke damage cone or extra XP is added. Native mob cast sources do not use player mana or player cooldowns; the encounter's AI cooldown owns the selection frequency. School power, resistances, L2 and other provider combat interactions still need joint balance testing.

This is an authored warning around a native instant spell. It does not implement Iron's `IMagicEntity`, synchronized caster animation, caster-mob goals, spell channels/recasts, potion drinking or native Counterspell cancellation of the warning. Actual hits interrupt it through the hurt timer and Epic Fight state. Long/channelled spells need a persistent native mob-casting integration before being added. The current addon pulses remain PvE player spells; their hostile-only target selection should not be reused as enemy attacks against players.

Remaining cooldown ticks save in the entity NBT and pause while the entity is unloaded. Unfinished warnings are discarded on reload without a projectile or cooldown refund. Missing legacy data uses the initial delay. Corrupt or oversized saved cooldowns clamp to 0–200. All provider classes remain behind loaded-mod guards; missing Iron's leaves the melee encounter available, and a failing adapter logs once and disables casting.

## Validation

Common JUnit tests cover release exactly once after 30 ticks, the 200-tick retry boundary, interruption/retargeting on the release tick, retained cooldown and bounded reload behavior. `verifyArpgIronsRuntime` checks the pinned native spell/casting API and writes `reports/irons-enemy-spell-api.txt`. CI also builds the actual NeoForge code and verifies Epic Fight assets and L2 profiles.

In a development world with Iron's and Epic Fight installed, create a fresh Sentinel using the tower demo. Stay at 4–12 blocks and check the initial delay, blue warning, locked aim, one native Icicle and ten-second attempt interval. Sidestep, interrupt with a melee hit or projectile, move behind cover, approach within four blocks, change the target, and kill the caster on the final warning tick; each must prevent release when ineligible. Check native spell damage/freezing and friendly-fire behavior with additional entities. Cross half health and verify the existing melee phase and boss bar resume. Unload/reload the chunk or restart during a warning: no pending projectile should release and recovery should remain. Check creative/spectator exclusions, disabled Icicle settings, each provider absent separately, and dedicated-server startup. Particle/pose/network timing and combined combat balance are not yet verified in Minecraft.

Further archetypes should have curated ability lists and separate selection rules: melee duelists, spellblades, ranged casters and support enemies. They should combine provider behavior rather than granting every spell or trait to every mob.
